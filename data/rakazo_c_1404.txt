### #1404 balabommablock-cpu [2026-10-20T11:34:31Z]
I think this one is mostly product, not model, so here's a small idea.

**Problem.** When something fails, the bot hands the problem straight back to the person ("can you check the key?"). After a few rounds, doing it yourself is faster. That's the moment people leave.

**Simpler fix.** One rule in the system prompt and the runner: *try twice before you ask, and never ask the same thing twice.*
1. On a failure, the bot first tries to find out why itself (read the error, test the credential, check the docs) before asking.
2. If it still has to ask, it asks once, with what it tried and the exact thing it needs.
3. The runner keeps a short list of questions already asked in this task. A repeat gets blocked and turned into "here's what I tried, here's where I'm stuck".

**What to cut.** Open-ended "please check X" asks. They should become one clear ask or nothing.

**How we'd know.** Count asks per finished task. If that number drops and finished tasks don't, it worked.

Happy to take a stab at step 3 if that sounds right.
